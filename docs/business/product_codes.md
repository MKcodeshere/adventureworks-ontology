# Product Coding Cheat Sheet

Internal wiki page used by the merchandising team. Codes appear in product
master data and in catalog exports.

## Product line

- **R** — Road bikes and road-focused gear. Built for paved surfaces and speed.
- **M** — Mountain bikes. Designed for off-road and trail use.
- **T** — Touring bikes. Built for long-distance travel with cargo capacity.
- **S** — Standard / accessories and clothing that are not bike-frame specific.

## Class (price / quality tier)

- **H** — High end
- **M** — Mid range
- **L** — Low / entry

## Style

- **W** — Women's
- **M** — Men's
- **U** — Universal / unisex

## Make flag

- true — manufactured in-house
- false — purchased from a vendor

## Example

"HL Mountain Frame — Black, 42" is a Mountain (M) product, High (H) class,
sold as a finished component of mountain bikes.
